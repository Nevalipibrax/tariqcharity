Who we are

Human dignity is where every good thing begins.

Tariq Charity Organization (TCO) is a community-based humanitarian and charitable organization headquartered in Mbale City, Uganda. We were established on the belief that compassion, solidarity and community development can help build a more hopeful future.

Our vision is a compassionate and empowered society where every person has access to basic human needs, education, clean water, healthcare, food, shelter and equal opportunities.

Trust is part of the mission

Good intentions.
Responsible action.

We believe donors and communities deserve openness, responsible stewardship and respect. These commitments are set out in the TCO constitution.

◎

Financial accountability

TCO's constitution provides for organizational bank accounts, proper accounting records, approved budgets, authorized expenditure and financial reporting.

◇

Donor transparency

Donations are to be used for their intended purposes. Project records, donor records and reports are part of the organization's required records.

♡

Safeguarding & dignity

TCO commits to protecting children and vulnerable adults, and prohibits abuse, exploitation, harassment, discrimination and neglect.
